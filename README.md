**Warung Kilat** is an original Indonesian street-food stall rush game. You are the cook of a small warung: grab ingredients from the pantry, chop them on the board (release in the yellow zone for a perfect chop), fry eggs and noodles in the wok, grill satay on the stove, brew tea, scoop rice and ice — then plate each dish in the exact recipe order and serve it at the window before the customer's patience runs out. Four original recipes unlock across a 180-second day, rush hour hits in the last 60 seconds, and on-time serves build a combo multiplier. All art is drawn on canvas and all music and sound effects are synthesized live with the Web Audio API — no files, no network, plain static files that play offline in a desktop browser.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD / arrow keys to move, E or Space to pick up and use, click a station to walk to it, hold E on the chopping board to chop, P to pause, M to mute, R to restart the day.
